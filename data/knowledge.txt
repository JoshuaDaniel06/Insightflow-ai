CUSTOMER REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase.

Products must be returned in unused condition with the original invoice.

Refund requests are reviewed by the customer support team.

Once a refund is approved, the refund is normally processed within 5 to 7 business days.

If the product is damaged or used, the refund request may be rejected.


PREMIUM CUSTOMER POLICY

Customers whose total purchase value is between 10000 and 14999 rupees are classified as Premium customers.

Premium customers receive priority customer support.

Premium customers may receive faster assistance for refund-related queries.


CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

Customer support is available for questions related to purchases, refunds, and account information.

Customers should provide their customer ID when requesting account-specific assistance.

The customer support team should verify customer information before providing account-specific details.


PURCHASE VALUE SEGMENTS

Customers with total purchases below 5000 rupees are classified as Low Value customers.

Customers with total purchases between 5000 and 9999 rupees are classified as Regular customers.

Customers with total purchases between 10000 and 14999 rupees are classified as Premium customers.

Customers with total purchases of 15000 rupees or more are classified as High Value customers.
